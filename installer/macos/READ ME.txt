ATMOSPHERIC (test build)
One sound, dealt by your sky.

INSTALL
1. Right-click "Install.command" and choose Open, then Open again.
   (macOS asks because this test build isn't signed by Apple yet.)
   If that's blocked, open Terminal, type   bash   and a space, drag
   Install.command into the window, and press Return.
2. Rescan plugins in your DAW. Atmospheric appears as an instrument
   (VST3 and Audio Unit). The standalone app is in ~/Applications.

PLAYING
- Five knobs start where today's weather put them (12 o'clock).
  Double-click a knob to return it to nature.
- Keep this Day saves today's sound to your Days list.
- Projects remember their Day: open the project tomorrow and it sounds
  the same. "Hear today" swaps in today's sky.
- Place: follow your connection's location, or fix a city.

ADMIN
- Cmd+Shift+G (or Cmd+W where the host allows, or triple-click the sky
  card) opens the globe. Click anywhere to hear that sky right now.

FILES
- Kept Days and settings: ~/Library/Application Support/Atmospheric/
- settings.json "relayUrl" overrides the weather server address.

UNINSTALL
- Run Uninstall.command.
